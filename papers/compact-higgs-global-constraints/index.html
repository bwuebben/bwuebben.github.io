Compact Higgs transitions

Global Constraints on Higgsing Localized Five-Dimensional Sectors in Compact M-Theory Vacua

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 7 September 2026 · Revised 26 November 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 44 pages · 505 KB

In the research guides: From compact geometry to coupled Higgs transitionsGeometry, gravity & information · Developing programme

Abstract

When several singularities of a compact Calabi–Yau threefold support five-dimensional field theories, a single bulk photon can gauge symmetries of several local sectors. We determine the resulting constraints on Higgs deformations for ordinary double points and anticanonical cones engineering the rank-one E₁, E₂, E₃, E₄ fixed points. On each choice of local Higgs components, the invariant coordinates attained on the rigid moment-map zero locus form the kernel of a matrix of divisor–curve pairings. For projective Calabi–Yau threefolds with these exact analytic germs and a smooth projective crepant resolution, small at the nodes and given by blowing up the cone vertices, the mixed smoothing theorem identifies this kernel with the global first-order deformation image on the chosen components. A simultaneous smoothing exists precisely when the kernel meets the complement of all local discriminants. We also determine the electric charge at a node that persists after a partial smoothing. In two compact examples, all thirty original nodes smooth and the same local deformation of a degree-six del Pezzo cone leaves one ordinary double point. Its linking two-sphere has unit period for a surviving bulk photon in the first example and zero period for every bulk photon in the second. The charge of the residual node thus distinguishes the two compact gaugings. The comparison determines a vector-sector observable on these geometric branches. Its identification on a prescribed microscopic Higgs vacuum at finite Planck mass remains open.
