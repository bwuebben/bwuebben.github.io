Stability and Geometric Degeneration · Paper 13

Metric wall crossing in rank-three K3 bundle moduli

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 6 November 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 21 pages · 428 KB

In the research guides: Stability and Geometric DegenerationGeometry & moduli · Integrated programme

Abstract

We determine the local harmonic L2 metric near a stability wall in a complex twelve-dimensional moduli space of rank-three bundles on a projective K3 surface. The central bundle is the sum of two copies of one spherical line bundle and a second spherical line bundle, with five-dimensional cross-extension spaces; the central Kähler metric is Ricci-flat. We compare the actual chamber metrics with the standard hyperkähler quotient metrics on T*Gr(2,5) and T*Gr(3,5), with relative error quadratic in the deformation size. The comparison is uniform through changes of stabilizer and permits unrelated rates of approach to the wall and its singular strata. It gives the exceptional-fibre collapse scale, complete pointed Gromov–Hausdorff limits after rescaling, quantitative matching to the wall metric, and intrinsic distance asymptotics to the contracted loci. The proof corrects the real moment equation within complex orbits with a bound that remains uniform at the reducible strata.
