Donaldson theory and the Atiyah–Floer program

Cobordism maps and Atiyah–Floer isomorphisms for framed instanton homology III

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 26 October 2026 · Revised 29 October 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 32 pages · 452 KB

In the research guides: Atiyah–Floer theory: from isomorphisms to functorialityKnots, four-manifolds & Floer theory · Developing programme

Abstract

We construct integral chain homotopy equivalences intertwining the usual framed instanton cobordism maps in the singular Hopf-link construction with symplectic cobordism maps oriented by transport from gauge theory. At common regular small data, the equivalences are represented by the Daemi–Fukaya–Lipyanskiy mixed count with a specified coherent orientation. For connected framed cobordisms, they are natural up to integral chain homotopy and one overall sign, with an explicit grading conversion, and extend by geometric changes of presentation. We also construct equivalences to the normalization in which attachment of a generating sphere in the surface moduli space preserves orientation, for every closed connected oriented three-manifold. Surgery generation gives a finite model built from one-generator complexes for the three-sphere. Counts through the three-sphere realize its grading-preserving matrix entries, so an algebraically chosen normalization becomes a finite integer linear combination of products of geometric counts. A polynomial in forward and reflected mixed counts transfers this construction to the symplectic complex. The proof uses the companion analytic and naturality results and the stated ordinary surgery and oriented gluing inputs. Identification with prescribed relative-spin maps and the nonsingular cap convention remains open.
