Categorical Kähler Geometry · Paper II

Extending the Kempf–Ness function to complexes of quiver representations

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 18 November 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 24 pages · 478 KB

In the research guides: Categorical Kähler GeometryGeometry & moduli · Integrated programme

Abstract

For every bounded complex of representations of a finite quiver with vertex charges in the upper half-plane, the superconnection endomorphism of Haiden, Katzarkov, Kontsevich and Pandit defines a closed one-form on the space of Hermitian metrics. We construct a primitive by transgression along the rescaling of the differentials. For nonzero total charge Z, rotating the primitive by Z―/|Z| and taking its imaginary part gives a real potential. On representations this potential is a positive multiple of the Kempf–Ness function and is nonincreasing along the flow defined by the argument of the unitary polar factor of the endomorphism. For a two-term complex in degrees zero and one whose differential surjects onto a term supported at a loopless vertex i, the potential is nonincreasing if the vertex charge zi satisfies argzi≥argZ and either argZ≥2π/5 or the differential has rank one. On a three-term Kronecker quiver complex the potential can increase even when the induced metric on its stable cohomology is harmonic. Quillen's superconnection gives the analogous potential for two-term complexes of holomorphic bundles on a compact Riemann surface. On a single bundle it is a positive multiple of Donaldson's functional.
