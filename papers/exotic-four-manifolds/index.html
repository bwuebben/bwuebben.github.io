Exotic four-manifolds

Toward an exotic S²×S² and an exotic ℂP²#ℂP̄²

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Working revision · 21 October 2026

Read on arXiv  ↗ PDF on this site · 56 pages · 624 KB

In the research guides: Two problems from the wider subjectKnots, four-manifolds & Floer theory

Abstract

We study a specified Luttinger filling in the construction of Lidman and Piccirillo whose simple connectivity would yield exotic S2×S2S^2\times S^2 and ℂℙ2#ℂℙ¯2\mathbb{CP}^2\#\overline{\mathbb{CP}}^2. An earlier version of this manuscript claimed simple connectivity from incorrectly derived group relations; that claim is withdrawn. We derive the complete fundamental-group presentation and express the group as a one-relator quotient of an iterated HNN extension containing the genus-two surface group. The filled group is trivial exactly when the final relation kills a specified fiber commutator. We also determine its universal central extension with cyclic kernel, construct geometric realizations of it, and extend the boundary involution over the filling. Proposed symplectic extensions construct a torus with a negative area–Maslov relation and exact tori detecting a nonzero transfer kernel for a choice of auxiliary handles. These constructions do not establish survival of the central element. The final filling problem remains unresolved.

The proposed geometric additions await independent mathematical verification. The final filling problem remains unresolved; the exotic homeomorphism conclusions are conditional.
