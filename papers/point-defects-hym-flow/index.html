Stability and Geometric Degeneration · Paper 32

Point defects and Hermitian–Yang–Mills flow on surfaces

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 12 November 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 33 pages · 501 KB

In the research guides: Stability and Geometric DegenerationGeometry & moduli · Integrated programme

Abstract

We determine the leading metric and concentration scales of Hermitian–Yang–Mills flow for locally free extensions 0→L→V→IWM→0 on a compact Kähler surface, where L,M are distinct line bundles of equal degree and W is a nonempty reduced finite scheme. Fix Hermitian–Einstein metrics on the line factors and their product as determinant. For every smooth initial metric with this determinant, the integrated squared norm Z(T) of the fixed inclusion satisfies Z(T)~v02/(4π2σT), where v0 is the volume and σ=∑p∈W(detQp)−1/2; Qp is the quadratic form of the inclusion norm at p in orthonormal coordinates. On the radius {Z(T)/(v0detQp)}1/2, the actual metrics converge smoothly to an explicit charge-one anti-self-dual metric. The inclusion half-height radius and the half-defect-energy radius have this same leading value. The proof corrects a compact family of metrics orthogonally to its small connection eigenspace and compares the resulting trajectory with every actual flow. It also gives a global inclusion profile and quantitative convergence outside shrinking balls. For arbitrary finite defects we prove Z(T)=O(T−1); if a defect is nonreduced, then TZ(T)→0.
