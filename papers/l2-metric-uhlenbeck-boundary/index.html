Stability and Geometric Degeneration · Paper 33

The L² metric at the Uhlenbeck boundary

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 13 November 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 22 pages · 413 KB

In the research guides: Stability and Geometric DegenerationGeometry & moduli · Integrated programme

Abstract

We construct local Kähler potentials for the L2 metric on moduli spaces of Hermitian–Yang–Mills connections over polarized projective surfaces that extend continuously to ideal connections. The potential combines a zeta determinant on a curve avoiding the bubble points with a weighted curvature integral and an explicit term for the defect cycle. On a projective K3 surface with a generic polarization and its Ricci-flat metric, let the Mukai vector be primitive, of rank at least two and positive square, with nonempty stable bundle locus. The potential identifies the L2 metric with the canonical singular Ricci-flat metric on the normalized Uhlenbeck compactification. Its completion is the Uhlenbeck compactification, and in complex dimension 2N its volume is (16π4VolX)N/N!. In rank two, the strata with stable residual bundle and distinct simple bubble points are totally geodesic hyperkähler orbifold strata, with the base-surface metric scaled by 4π2. At stable-residual boundary points, we identify the affine tangent cones and their Reeb actions and compute their volume densities from instanton partition functions.
