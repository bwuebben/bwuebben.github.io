Universal curve counting

The first variation and the one-mark coefficient of circle exchange for balanced local curves

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 26 November 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 21 pages · 421 KB

In the research guides: Universal curve countingCalabi–Yau transitions · Integrated programme

Abstract

For a balanced local curve of positive genus we prove a first-order circle-exchange identity for the two stable-pair indices over its Picard variety. After their common determinant normalization, the indices agree modulo (1−κ)2 in integral family K-theory in every curve degree and both counting gradings, where κ is the threefold volume character. At positive exchanged grading the first variation is a sum over hooks of Adams operations on the derived pushforwards of powers of the Poincaré line twisted by powers of the canonical bundle. With κ unrestricted, the entire coefficient of exchanged grading one agrees separately for each Young diagram and is given by a sum over its removable boxes. The first comparison deforms the graded moment equations to walls with symmetric powers of the curve as fixed loci. The second evaluates a product of two Macdonald currents and descends the resulting disk identity to the Picard family.
