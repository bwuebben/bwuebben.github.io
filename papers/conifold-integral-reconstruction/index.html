Stability and Geometric Degeneration · Paper 31

Integral vanishing cycles from periods on conifold smoothings

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 12 November 2026 · Revised 13 November 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 37 pages · 505 KB

In the research guides: Stability and Geometric DegenerationGeometry & moduli · Integrated programme

Abstract

We give a terminating construction of the marked integral vanishing-cycle map for a six-parameter family of Calabi–Yau conifold smoothings in P3×P3. The eight vanishing spheres span a primitive rank-six lattice whose symplectic normal form is determined by topology. We recover their coordinates in an independent geometric marking by constructing an effective neighbourhood in vanishing-period coordinates and absolute error bounds for cycle transport and integration. Picard–Lefschetz period jumps determine the integral columns and monodromy without an a priori bound on the integer entries or restrictions on nonzero period ratios. Recovery at one reference fibre suffices throughout the smooth locus. As applications, we verify the hypotheses of established analytic results on the same fibres in a possibly ineffective smaller neighbourhood. Over R, the kernel of the recovered map is the image of divisor restriction to the exceptional curves; its capacity-weighted quadratic form and limiting harmonic mass give relative asymptotics for the two small positive Ricci-flat two-form eigenvalues. For the constructed nonflat polystable rank-four bundles, a local invariant manifold of Hermitian–Yang–Mills metric flow is tangent at the Hermitian–Einstein metric to the complete low spectral subspace of the Laplacian on trace-free Hermitian endomorphisms, including its kernel.
