Instanton homology of knots

The S-complex differential of torus knots

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 2 October 2026 · Revised 6 October 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 12 pages · 412 KB

Abstract

For a torus knot K=T(p,q) the traceless SU(2) character variety is finite and nondegenerate, so Daemi and Scaduto’s S-complex for singular instanton homology can be built on it. The resulting complex has rank 1+|σ(K)|, where σ is the signature, and by theorems of Li and Ye and of Kronheimer and Mrowka its homology I♮(K) has rank ‖ΔK‖1, the sum of the absolute values of the coefficients of the Alexander polynomial; so the differential has rank R(p,q)=12(1+|σ|−‖Δ‖1). We express R(p,q) through floor sums and show that, for coprime 2≤p<q, R(p,q)≥⌊(p−2b)2/4⌋, where b∈{1,…,p−1} is the inverse of q modulo p, and that the differential vanishes if and only if p=2, or p is odd and q≡2 or q≡p+2(−1)(p−1)/2(mod2p); the ‘if’ direction is due to Daemi and Scaduto. By a result of Daemi and Scaduto, the component of the differential to the reducible generator is nonzero over ℚ exactly when their invariant h is positive. We show that the components from degree 3 to degree 2 and from degree 1 to degree 0 have ranks ⌊R/2⌋ and ⌈R/2⌉, independently of the choices in the construction. In particular the ℤ/4-graded group I♮(T(p,q);ℚ) depends only on σ and ‖Δ‖1, and we give it explicitly. In each small-perturbation pillowcase computation of Hedden, Herald and Kirk, a bigon ends at the reducible generator exactly when h>0. We formulate a chain-level conjecture identifying the two complexes over F2; it predicts this pattern whenever the map to the reducible is nonzero modulo 2.
