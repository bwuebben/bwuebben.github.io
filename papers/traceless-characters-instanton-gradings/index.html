Instanton homology of knots

Traceless SU(2) characters and ℤ/4 instanton gradings for two-bridge and (3,n)-torus knots

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 27 July 2026 · Revised 6 October 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 15 pages · 419 KB

Abstract

For every torus knot, the rank of the differential of the Daemi–Scaduto S-complex of singular instanton homology is determined by the signature and the Alexander polynomial; this follows from theorems of Li–Ye and Daemi–Scaduto. For the torus knots T(3,n)T(3,n) the differential has rank zero or one according to nn modulo 66, and its rank equals Daemi and Scaduto’s Frøyshov-type invariant; they proved this in two of the four residue classes and for n=4,5n=4,5, and Hedden, Herald and Kirk computed it for n≤38n\le38. Following Lewallen, Klassen and Hedden–Herald–Kirk, we describe the generators of the instanton complexes and their ℤ/4\mathbb{Z}/4 gradings for two-bridge knots, whose irreducible traceless characters are all binary dihedral, and for T(3,n)T(3,n), where at most one is. We also give a proof through Khovanov homology, using Schütz’s computation for three-braids, of Li and Ye’s computation of the rank of the instanton homology of T(3,n)T(3,n), and compare the differential of the knot 8198_{19} with the single pillowcase bigon of Hedden, Herald and Kirk.
