Stability and Geometric Degeneration · Paper 27

Hermitian–Yang–Mills flow on degenerating Calabi–Yau threefolds of type K

Bernd Johannes Wuebben ORCID ↗

Preprint · 11 November 2026

Read paper (PDF)  ↗ 28 pages · 475 KB

In the research guides: Stability and Geometric DegenerationGeometry & moduli · Integrated programme

Abstract

We construct a rank-four holomorphic bundle family on a compact degeneration, with smoothing parameter t, of Enriques Calabi–Yau threefolds. The threefolds are free quotients of products of a K3 surface and an elliptic curve degenerating to a cycle of four rational curves. On the unitary gluing locus, the bundles have nonflat Hermitian–Einstein connections for prescribed product-induced Kähler metrics, which need not be Ricci-flat. We identify all five small eigenvalues of the trace-free endomorphism Laplacian, prove relative asymptotics uniform up to strict polystability, and establish a uniform complementary gap. The effective quadratic energy lies on an interval with one-dimensional endpoint constraints whose relative angle records holonomy around the elliptic central-fibre cycle. We derive a finite-dimensional gradient flow of noncommuting positive Hermitian matrices and reconstruct comparison metrics approximating actual Hermitian–Yang–Mills trajectories in global L2 affine distance. With determinant fixed to that of the Hermitian–Einstein reference metric, an open class of initial metrics whose relative logarithms are globally bounded and whose component-exterior restrictions admit uniformly bounded C6,β extensions to the normalization components, 0<β<1, enters this description after physical time s=o(L), where L=log(1/|t|). Tracking is uniform on bounded s/L intervals and, where the least effective eigenvalue is bounded below by a positive constant, for all subsequent times. Within the specified family, the five eigenvalues, elliptic component areas and known normalization L asymptotically recover the projective conjugacy class of the cycle transport. Infinite physical-time limits need not commute with degeneration. The rescaled limits commute under this gap bound when the selected parallel limits of the component flows and the gluing parameters converge, but can fail near reducibility.
