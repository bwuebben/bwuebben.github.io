MATHEMATICAL FRAMING

A precise model, with visible limits.

A Calabi–Yau threefold has complex dimension three and real dimension six. The canvas shows core cycles, projections, and an explicitly specified real slice. It does not render an entire compact threefold or compute a Ricci-flat metric.

The local model

Σ zⱼ² = μ,   z = x + iy

For real μ > 0, x·y = 0 and |x|² − |y|² = μ. Set u = x/|x|: y is tangent to the unit S³, giving the cotangent-bundle topology. The zero-section y = 0 is the vanishing sphere of radius √μ in the quadric coordinates.

The slice sets z₁ = u, z₂ = v, z₃ = iw, z₄ = 0, with u,v,w real; it satisfies u² + v² − w² = μ. At μ = 0 its drawing is a real two-dimensional double cone, not the six-dimensional conifold.

The small resolution is modelled by det A = xy − zw = 0, A = [[x,z],[w,y]], together with a kernel line or cokernel line in P¹. At A = 0 the fibre is all of P¹. The area A in the control is a separate Kähler parameter; it is not a continuation of the complex smoothing μ.

The animations

The topology movie depicts local exceptional curves collapsing to nodes, followed by vanishing spheres appearing on the smoothing. Endpoint Hodge numbers remain integers and are not interpolated. The tropical movie traces the belt, sketches the chain lifts and highlights coefficient support. Moving dots and changing diagram depth are visual guides, not a computed metric or a continuous deformation between mirror spaces.

The projections

The S³ view samples actual Hopf fibres on the unit sphere in C² and stereographically projects them into R³, scaling the picture by √μ. Only a finite collection of fibres is drawn. The S² view displays the exceptional core with latitude/longitude curves. Camera perspective is a visual aid.

Example data and attribution

Bernd Johannes Wuebben, Tropical 2-cycles and relations among vanishing spheres in Batyrev mirror families, manuscript dated September 25, 2026; §11.1, §11.2 and Table 1 of the manuscript. X₉’s circuit matrix and all three example bases are transcribed from those sections. Diagram layouts are schematic and do not reproduce the complete affine base or prove the lifting theorem. Relative tropical cycles supply the mirror 4-chain. The explicit tropical 3-chain lift on the resolution uses the stricter CBM cycle conditions; membership in K always gives an abstract homology relation there. The manuscript’s stated results are illustrated under its hypotheses, rather than independently verified here.

X₉ uses direct coefficients to test the circuit equation. X₁₉ and X₂₀ use combinations of the listed basis vectors, so those selected vectors always lie in K. A zero in one selected vector is different from a coordinate forced to be zero throughout K.
