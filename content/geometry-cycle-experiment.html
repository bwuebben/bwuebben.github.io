The formulas and the geometric scope

In the chamber with stability levels (a, −a−b, b), a,b > 0, the harmonic areas are πa, πb and π(a+b). The plane volumes are π²a²/2 and π²b²/2. Ambient diameters are comparable to √a and √b with constants independent of a/b. When a/b → 0 and a+b → 0, rescaling at the smaller plane gives the complete Calabi metric on T*ℙ²; the reversed hierarchy gives the corresponding limit at the other plane. No numerical radius of validity for the common scale ε is supplied by these controls.

The boundary group is part of the integral attachment calculation for these two intersecting planes. Reconstructing the compact moduli space's cohomology also requires the marked complement and its restriction map; the displayed matrix alone does not supply those data.
