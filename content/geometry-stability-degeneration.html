Stability walls and geometric degeneration Integrated programme

From realizable chambers to metrics and flows.

The geometric route

Begin with the toric tangent stability papers, then local stability chambers. Continue to nonsimplicial tangent chambers, which constructs genuine weak-Fano examples and determines their accessible slope parameters.

The analytic route

Begin with eigenvalues near stability walls, then uniform spectra for bipartite extensions. The earlier HYM-flow paper supplies the dynamical entry point.

Where the routes meet

Metric response and Hermitian Yang–Mills flow for two-step extensions brings the independently proved geometry and spectral mechanism together with metric-response, inverse and actual-flow results on the same tangent bundles. The spectral paper does not depend on these particular examples, and the geometric paper does not depend on the spectral analysis.

The broader programme asks for repeated-factor and higher-step extensions, singular graded objects and moduli-metric degeneration. Those require further theorems. Cycles can retain harmonic extension data, and flows can retain initial-metric information.
