Stability and Geometric Degeneration Integrated programme

From realizable chambers to metrics, moduli and flows.

Can finite deformation and symmetry data predict how geometry loses rigidity? The programme connects realizable algebraic stability chambers, small Dolbeault eigenvalues, Hermitian–Einstein metrics and geometric flows, with relative estimates that remain meaningful when positive scales vanish at very different rates.

The two-step theory joins these descriptions on the same weak-Fano toric tangent bundles. Later papers treat specified three-step extensions, repeated factors, local moduli metrics and initial-condition-sensitive dynamics. A general rank-two surface theorem shows that two proposed bounded-remainder metric asymptotics require modification for singular graded quotients, even away from the singularities. It includes nonreduced zeros and repeated factors, with toric and K3 examples. The general theory and the replacement singular growth law remain open.
