The geometric route

Begin with the toric tangent stability papers, then local stability chambers. Continue to nonsimplicial tangent chambers, which constructs genuine weak-Fano examples and determines their accessible slope parameters. The revised paper gives a positive-spanning criterion without local freeness and an explicit Calabi–Yau conifold transition on its fourfold. The blowup-and-bundle paper now includes an exact census of all 6,281 Picard-number-ten eightfolds; every strictly semistable case is a product.

The analytic route

Begin with eigenvalues near stability walls, then uniform spectra for bipartite extensions. Continue to spectra with repeated stable factors: the full matrix multiplicity algebra controls every small mode, and comparison with the actual Hermitian–Einstein metric remains uniform when positive scales separate arbitrarily. A fixed rank-five bundle realizes six small modes. The earlier HYM-flow paper supplies the dynamical entry point.

Where the routes meet

Metric response and Hermitian Yang–Mills flow for two-step extensions brings the independently proved geometry and spectral mechanism together with metric-response, inverse and actual-flow results on the same tangent bundles. The spectral paper does not depend on these particular examples, and the geometric paper does not depend on the spectral analysis.

Repeated-factor stationary spectra are established for the specified simple locally free two-step extensions. Quantitative response and actual flow with repeated factors, higher-step extensions, singular graded objects and higher-dimensional bundle moduli metrics require further theorems. Cycles can retain harmonic extension data, and flows can retain initial-metric information.

The next questions

Can the projection estimate yield a general small-gauge correction theorem without a lower bound on the positive finite Hessian spectrum? A broader spectral theorem would also need geometric control of the coupling between the limiting kernel and its gapped complement. Establishing both estimates in a new geometric setting would turn the present mechanism into a more general moment-map theory.

A genuine three-step extension is a demanding test: composable arrows satisfy integrability relations, and removing blocks across a projection can destroy those relations. These are research directions, distinct from the completed two-step theorem; parameter derivatives and actual flow require additional estimates.
