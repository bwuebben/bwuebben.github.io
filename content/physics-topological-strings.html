Topological strings and curve counting Developing programme

One variable counts curves and measures a rotation.

The membrane proposal of Nekrasov and Okounkov gives a geometric interpretation of the topological-string variable. Choose one circle on a Calabi–Yau fivefold and the variable grades the Euler characteristic of a stable pair. Choose the other and it measures how a plane rotates. A balanced local curve gives a setting in which both calculations can be done.

What the two circles fix

The first fixes a threefold built from two line bundles of equal degree over a smooth curve of positive genus. The second fixes the curve times a plane. The two stable-pair indices agree as integral classes on their common space of cycles, in every positive curve degree and as the line bundles vary over the Picard variety.

Where the partition function comes from

On the product with a plane, a deformation of the matrix equations eliminates the virtual contribution above minimum Euler characteristic. At the minimum, rotation weights on Hilbert schemes of points in the plane give the balanced local-curve partition function.

What varies over the curve family

For a doubled genus-two curve, the first variation at the lowest Euler characteristic agrees in the two descriptions. At exchanged grading two its Chern character is twelve times the principal polarization of the Picard variety. The class vanishes on each individual target.

The refined circle-exchange theorem compares the determinant-normalized indices to first order in the volume character in every positive curve degree and both counting gradings. At exchanged grading one, equality holds for arbitrary volume character, separately for each Young diagram. The quadratic and cubic coefficients likewise agree with the volume character free, throughout the Picard family and at every Euler characteristic.

For the doubled genus-two curve the connected index is supported at coincident transverse points. Whether two wrapped branes form a normalizable five-dimensional particle when they can separate remains open.
