Four-manifolds

Two problems from the wider subject.

Neither paper below is a programme of its own. Each contributes to a problem whose centre lies elsewhere, and each states plainly how far it gets.

Toward the Donaldson–Seiberg–Witten comparison

Feehan and Leness set out to derive the relation between Donaldson and Seiberg–Witten invariants from moduli spaces of SO(3) monopoles, in which both theories appear as strata of one space. One-bubble neighborhoods constructs an oriented neighbourhood of the first Uhlenbeck level under stated regularity hypotheses, supplying analytic input that their level-one link pairing had deferred.

Collision strata, zero-spinor solutions and levels two and higher remain further problems. The applications are conditional and do not give an unconditional comparison.

An exotic S² × S²?

Following Lidman and Piccirillo, the four-manifold manuscript constructs a symplectic manifold with the cohomology ring of S² × S² that is not diffeomorphic to it. If it is simply connected, it is an exotic S² × S². That reduces to one question in combinatorial group theory: does a single commutator lie in a given normal closure? Neither answer is proved.

The manuscript is a working revision. An earlier version claimed simple connectivity from incorrectly derived relations; that claim is withdrawn, and the newest sections await independent verification.
