Knot complexes and the pillowcase.

Cut a knot complement along a sphere that meets the knot in four points. The flat connections on the punctured sphere form the pillowcase, and the two halves of the complement trace immersed curves in it. The question is whether the Floer homology of those curves reproduces the singular instanton homology of the knot, and at what level of structure.

What is proved

For the torus knots T(3,n), the pillowcase complex is chain homotopy equivalent over 𝔽₂ to the Daemi–Scaduto complex of singular instanton homology. For the hyperbolic pretzel knots P(−2,3,q) the two homologies agree as ℤ/4-graded groups. The comparisons are exact: the curves, their intersections and the gradings are computed for each family.

The missing bridge

A different decomposition needs bounding cochains: with none, the pairing has rank 7 where instanton homology has rank 9. Constructing the cochains of Cazassus, Herald, Kirk and Kotelskiy where zero cochains give the wrong rank, and proving that they restore instanton homology, is the open problem, together with the chain-level identification for all torus knots. The bounding-cochain paper is conditional on Conjecture D of Cazassus, Herald, Kirk and Kotelskiy, and says so.
