Three ways to ask what geometry remembers

Study a limit. Recover a space.

A moduli space is a space whose points represent geometric objects. Moving through it changes a bundle, a metric or a solution of an equation. At special points the object acquires symmetries, cycles contract, or a solution breaks into pieces. Those changes expose structure that is difficult to see in the smooth region.
