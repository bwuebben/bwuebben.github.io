Geometry & moduli

Which geometric structure can we recover?

A moduli space is a space whose points represent geometric objects. Moving through it changes a bundle, a metric or a solution of an equation. At special points the object acquires symmetries, cycles contract, or a solution breaks into pieces. Other routes start from a metric on a complex of vector spaces or the symmetries of a toric variety. Each reveals algebraic information that can be read from geometric data.
