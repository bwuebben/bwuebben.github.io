03 / A little vocabulary

Six ideas that connect the papers.

Stability walls

Stability compares the slope—degree divided by rank—of a bundle with its subsheaves. A wall is a parameter value where slopes become equal, so the type of canonical solution can change.

Canonical metrics and flow

A Hermitian–Einstein metric solves the bundle’s canonical curvature equation. Hermitian–Yang–Mills (HYM) metric flow evolves a metric towards that equation; approaching a wall can introduce very slow directions.

Small eigenvalues

These identify directions with weak restoring force. Relative estimates track even the weakest mode when several rates approach zero at very different speeds.

Moduli spaces

A moduli space parametrizes geometric objects up to isomorphism. Its harmonic L² metric measures the size of a deformation by integrating over the underlying manifold.

Finite models and symmetries

Graphs and quivers encode component or extension data. Quotienting by their symmetries gives finite models for particular infinite-dimensional metric and flow problems.

Degenerating bases

The underlying curve or threefold can itself develop singularities. Thin necks and collapsing cycles create additional small modes, so a theory with a fixed base needs further estimates.

What the ranking means

This ordering is retrospective: later generalizations can reduce an earlier paper’s standalone scope without erasing the originality of its method. A physics-focused ranking would give Paper 21 more weight.

What an arrow actually says

A solid arrow marks an expressly used theorem or geometric construction. A dashed arrow marks an acknowledged method extension; it does not mean the later paper imports an unproved result. An input may support one application, rather than the whole later paper.

The 44 selected arrows have source excerpts in the paper inspector. They are not an exhaustive citation graph, are not transitively reduced, and omit classical and external-series inputs. Dotted thematic links are optional and never counted as dependencies.

Scope, versions and evidence

All 28 papers are public preprints. Links open the website’s current paper pages and verified PDF mirrors. The ranking and selected dependency graph reflect the manuscripts dated or revised through 11 November 2026; each paper page records its original date, current version and archive DOI.

The ranking is an editorial judgment, not a citation score or a forecast of journal reception. An arrow records an identified use or extension; it does not certify every proof in either paper.
