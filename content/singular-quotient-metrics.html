Singular quotient metrics

Metrics when stabilizers change.

The quotient-metric paper asks how the geometry of a moduli space compares with its finite-dimensional model when symmetry increases. Near a reducible Yang–Mills connection on a closed surface, it proves relative estimates for the actual quotient metric and every small gauge eigenvalue, including changes of kernel dimension and arbitrarily separated vanishing scales. It also computes the leading metric correction.

The connection with Hermitian–Einstein spectral degeneration is the infinitesimal orbit operator. Explicit quaternionic projective and hyperbolic reductions provide a comparison with the finite-Planck Higgs quotient problem. The general quaternionic Kähler comparison across stabilizer changes and higher-dimensional HYM quotient metrics remain open directions.
