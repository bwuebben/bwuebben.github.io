Equivariant Quantum Geometry Integrated programme

From quantum multiplication to exceptional objects.

Inversion on the torus of the projective line exchanges the two points added at its ends. Yet it fixes the classes of both the structure sheaf and the hyperplane line bundle in integral K-theory. The mirror function x + 1/x shows where to look for the labels of those classes. Its critical points, +1 and −1, are each fixed by inversion.

Every smooth projective complex toric Fano variety admits a semisimple quantum parameter fixed by the automorphisms of its fan. At this parameter quantum cohomology splits into one-dimensional factors. At each admissible lifted phase the factors label a full exceptional collection, a sequence of objects generating the derived category of coherent sheaves. The fan group permutes its members, and their individual K-classes give the integral Gamma asymptotic basis. Even when two Euler eigenvalues coincide, distinct objects with that value have vanishing morphisms in both directions in every degree.

In characteristic zero, Galois orbits of the objects give division-algebra components on every form of the variety. On the nonarchimedean fixed quantum base, over the locus with the maximal number of distinct Euler eigenvalues, the primitive-idempotent cover modulo the fan group gives the reduced Euler cover. Its connected components are the local equivariant Hodge atoms. Fan symmetries and exceptional collections on toric Fano varieties develops both consequences.
