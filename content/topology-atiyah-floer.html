Atiyah–Floer functoriality.

The Atiyah–Floer conjecture predicts that two Floer theories of a three-manifold agree: one counts instantons on a four-dimensional cylinder, the other counts holomorphic curves in a space of flat connections on a surface that splits the manifold in two. Daemi, Fukaya and Lipyanskiy proved an isomorphism of this kind for framed instanton homology. The programme asks what lies beyond the isomorphism of groups: whether it carries the maps induced by cobordisms, and the operations of the splitting surface, from one theory to the other.

What is proved

The isomorphism intertwines the cobordism maps of connected cobordisms: over 𝔽₂ in the homotopy category of chain complexes, and over ℤ up to one sign for each cobordism, with orientations transported from gauge theory. The third paper joins this comparison to the usual framed instanton maps in their singular Hopf-link realization, up to an explicit grading conversion and one sign, and constructs equivalences between the two orientation systems of the Daemi–Fukaya–Lipyanskiy construction for every closed connected oriented three-manifold. In the embedded-Lagrangian setting, the point operation is respected only after a correction of 8, 4 and 0 in genus one, two and higher, produced by bubbles on the matching line.

What remains

Two prescribed conventions have not been compared with the transported orientations: Scaduto's nonsingular cap convention, and relative-spin systems fixed in advance on the Lagrangian correspondences. The point operation has been compared in a different setting from the cobordism maps, and a comparison that respects compositions coherently at chain level has not been attempted.
