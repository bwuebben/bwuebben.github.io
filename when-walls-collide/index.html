The question

The start and finish are the same. Should the answer depend on which side of the joint you take? The missing information sits exactly where the two operations interact.

Open FJRW theory studies invariants of marked disks for a polynomial such as x⁴ + y⁴. Those invariants depend on boundary conditions. Changing the conditions acts on a potential, a polynomial whose coefficients record the invariants with their prescribed signs.

Here we follow one exact example with two distinct labels. Their variables satisfy t₁² = t₂² = 0, while t₁t₂ survives. A single label sees each wall separately. The product t₁t₂ detects their interaction.

The exact example, operators, and path conventions

Work in Q[x,y,t₁,t₂]/(t₁²,t₂²). The labels have twists (1,0) and (0,1), both with descendent degree one. Put Xa,b = xᵃyᵇ((b+1)x∂x − (a+1)y∂y), Σ₁ = exp(−t₁X₁₀/2), Σ₂ = exp(−t₂X₀₁/2), and Uc = exp(ct₁t₂X₁₁). Each exponential terminates after its linear term in this ring.

Wmin = x⁴ + y⁴ − 4t₁xy⁴ − 2t₂y⁵ + 12t₁t₂xy⁵. At the forward endpoints, write V(a,b) = x⁴ + y⁴ − 2t₁x⁵ − 4t₂x⁴y + t₁t₂(a xy⁵ + b x⁵y). The two numbers are potential coefficients, not an unqualified count of disks.

The supports have normals (3,4), (3,2), and (1,1); the mixed support is a ray in s₂ ≥ 0. The horizontal routes have s₂ = ±1/10, with common endpoints (−1/4,0) and (1/4,0). Their end connectors miss every support. These are shortened versions of the paper’s local test routes, with the same crossings and signs. The drawn axes use a common scale.

The ribbon lists crossings chronologically. An operator word acts rightmost first: the corrected upper route is Σ₂UcΣ₁, and the lower is Σ₁Σ₂. Reverse travel starts at that route’s own endpoint and applies the inverse crossings, recovering Wmin.

Why checking x and y is enough here
The transformations fix t₁ and t₂ and are algebra automorphisms. Agreement on x and y therefore gives agreement on the entire coefficient ring. The comparison uses exact rational coefficients; no numerical tolerance is involved.
[X₁₀, X₀₁] = −3X₁₁, and Σ₂Σ₁ = U3/4Σ₁Σ₂. Thus Σ₂U−3/4Σ₁ = Σ₁Σ₂. On Wmin, the upper endpoint is V(2−8c,10+8c), and the lower is V(8,4).
Paper 2 organizes transformations into factors ordered by the slopes of their boundary rays in each finite truncation. The boundary ray for Xk₁,k₂ is spanned by (k₁+1,k₂+1); it is not automatically a direction in an affine base. The quartic paper supplies the cofactor placement used here. Local versus global
The correction wall settles a local disagreement. It does not make the surface’s global holonomy disappear. For this core loop, K has trace 18 and eigenvalues 9 ± 4√5.

 
What comes from the annular construction
The affine base disk has singular points A, B, C. The parity character is 1 around A and C, and 0 around B. Cutting from A to C and gluing two copies crosswise gives an annulus. Its four singular points are Ã, B₀, B₁, C̃; the two lifts of B are focus–focus, while the branched germs at Ã and C̃ are different. The source figure’s relative placement is retained in the annulus view.
The selected core-loop class is γCγA−1, based in sheet S₀. On the exponent lattice in the source’s F-coordinates it acts by K = [[−1,2],[−10,19]]. Its determinant is one. Other loops in the punctured annulus can have different holonomies, even if they become freely homotopic after forgetting the singular points.
The animation, where enabled, marks progress around the loop. The returned matrix is applied at the end of a circuit; no interpolated affine connection is being computed. A tangent-frame representation would use the inverse transpose. The two boundaries and the marked core curve are drawn schematically, not as a Euclidean metric model of the affine structure.

A correction can be imposed.
Apply the algebraic normal form to carry V(8,4) to V(0,12). The finite operation is explicit. Its intrinsic geometric origin is the open question.

 
Where the explorer reaches the research frontier
Can the surface supply its own correction?
The missing construction is an operation intrinsic to the annulus, at its boundary, that produces the required normal form. Replacing a wall coefficient by the GKT value does not by itself solve the annular consistency problem.

 
The discrepancy, period class, and imposed normal form
Annular transport gives Wtr = V(8,4), whereas Wmax = V(0,12). The two-label GKT transition is Σ₂U1/4Σ₁; the annular path word is Σ₂U−3/4Σ₁. They differ by U₁, so U₁Wtr = Wmax.
The relevant t₁t₂ period class of V(a,b) is (6 − (a+b)/2)[xy], which vanishes for both endpoints. Here [xy] abbreviates the class of xy dx∧dy for the twisted differential ℏd + d(x⁴+y⁴)∧, after extracting the t₁t₂ coefficient of the exponential deformation. In the paper, the discrepancy has the relative primitive β = t₁t₂d(x²y²). This exact finite comparison explains why the period test loses information; it does not say that every observable or every period problem is identical.
UcV(a,b) = V(a−8c,b+8c), and the maximal normal form is Ua/8V(a,b) = V(0,a+b). The final control applies this algebraic operation externally. It does not implement the missing boundary construction.
