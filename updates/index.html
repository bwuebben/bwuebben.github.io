27 October 2026

Small Dolbeault eigenvalues near stability walls

A new paper determines every small Hermitian–Einstein endomorphism eigenvalue near intersecting stability walls, with a uniform relative estimate across independent slope gaps. It includes a second-order two-factor expansion and toric examples with arbitrarily many spectral scales. The manuscript and exact matrix computations are public, with a Zenodo archive.

26 October 2026

Local stability chambers for toric tangent bundles

A new paper classifies tangent stability for a family of toric projective bundles, constructs local chambers with arbitrarily many independent boundaries, and determines nearby stable polarizations in the 31 indecomposable semistable census cases. Includes the full manuscript and exact computations.
