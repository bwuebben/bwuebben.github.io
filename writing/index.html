Essays & perspectives

On knowledge,
structure, and explanation.

A description can be accurate and still leave out what matters: the shape of a drum, the risks a track record was paid for, the relation that decides whether a space can be smoothed. These essays follow that gap through markets, geometry, physics and the institutions now being rebuilt around machines. Where a model sharpens the argument, the essay builds one and states its limits beside it.
