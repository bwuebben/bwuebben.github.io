Bernd Johannes Wuebben

Markets · AI · Mathematics · Theoretical Physics · International Relations

I work on the cross-section of returns — how systematic signals are built, validated, and turned into portfolios — chiefly in corporate credit and fixed income, with a parallel interest in the spectral structure of factor models and in what statistical power implies about how much alpha can survive in equilibrium.

Separately, and for its own sake, I work in geometric topology and algebraic geometry: four-manifolds and instanton Floer theory, mirror symmetry, and the geometry of toric Fano varieties and Calabi–Yau threefolds.

A third strand runs through black hole information and random-matrix universality, and through what large-scale artificial intelligence does to markets, institutions, and the balance of power between states.

Research

  • Systematic investing — signal combination, shrinkage, and the continuum between diversification and mean–variance optimization.
  • Asset pricing — the spectral structure of the cross-section, identification of priced risk versus statistical alpha, the equilibrium level of factor compensation.
  • AI and the machine economy — institutional cognition, prediction markets as informational infrastructure, and what changes once hypotheses are free.
  • International relations — how large-scale AI deployment reshapes deterrence, the attribution of intent, and material power.
  • Geometric topology — exotic smooth structures on four-manifolds, instanton knot homology, the Atiyah–Floer program.
  • Algebraic geometry — mirror symmetry, stability of tangent bundles on toric Fanos, deformations and smoothability of Calabi–Yau threefolds, open FJRW theory.
  • Theoretical physics — quantum gravity, holography, black hole thermodynamics and the information paradox, quantum information, random-matrix theory and free probability, critical phenomena and universality.
