Finance · Mathematics · Theoretical Physics

Bernd Johannes
Wuebben

A price is a claim about the future that somebody was willing to fund. My research in finance asks what can be known about such claims: whether a model that earns is a model that explains, where compensation for risk ends and compensation for absorbing other people’s constraints begins, and what remains hidden in a record of success.

These questions lead outward: toward who is on the other side of a trade and what they are solving for, and toward markets as aggregation mechanisms that sometimes aggregate belief and sometimes only aggregate attention.

In mathematics and theoretical physics, I pursue questions about geometry, topology, and information: how local structures constrain a whole, what survives a change of description, and how much of an object its invariants can reveal. My particular interests include four-manifolds, gauge and Floer theory, mirror symmetry, and moduli spaces.

This site collects my research papers and essays, including work on AI, institutions, and international order.

Markets and quantitative finance

The Validation Bottleneck: Alpha Discovery When Hypotheses Are Free

Language models have made investment hypotheses free. The evidence that can judge them still arrives one year per year, and the paper works out what that does to discovery.

Smoothability of Calabi–Yau threefolds

Mirror Symmetry for Conifold Relations in Calabi–Yau Threefolds

Mirror symmetry trades relations among exceptional curves for relations among vanishing spheres. The paper proves the trade exact, as an equality of integral lattices, for specified Batyrev families near the maximally degenerate limit.

Black hole information

The Page Curve Is Not Enough: Universality, Ensemble Dependence, and Microscopic Ambiguity in Black Hole Information Recovery

An entropy curve can agree exactly while the ensembles behind it fluctuate differently and the channels behind it differ in what they keep. The paper builds exact finite examples of both.

Markets and quantitative finance

The AI-Native Asset Manager: Institutional Cognition as Competitive Advantage

When analysis is cheap, the scarce resource is the architecture by which a firm generates, challenges, sizes and learns from its beliefs. The paper designs an investment firm around it.

Donaldson theory and the Atiyah–Floer program

Cobordism maps and Atiyah–Floer isomorphisms for framed instanton homology I

Daemi, Fukaya and Lipyanskiy identified framed instanton homology with a Lagrangian Floer homology. The paper proves that such identifications also respect four-dimensional cobordism maps, over the field of two elements.

Compact Higgs transitions

The Higgs Branch of a Compact Calabi–Yau Transition at Finite Planck Mass

Without gravity, a Higgs branch comes with a dial that can smooth its singularity. With gravity the dial disappears, and the paper shows that the singularity keeps its shape, under stated freeness and regularity hypotheses.

01

Markets & inference

What can a successful model actually tell us?

A model that earns has not thereby explained anything. The papers ask what discovery means once language models make hypotheses free while evidence still arrives one year per year, how a portfolio should respond to a forecast, and how a firm should form, test and revise its beliefs.

02

Knots, four-manifolds & Floer theory

What survives when we cut a space apart?

Cut a space along a surface and a hard question becomes a comparison between simpler pieces. The papers compare two Floer theories, one built from instantons and one from holomorphic curves, exactly for families of knots and for the maps that four-dimensional cobordisms induce.

03

Calabi–Yau transitions

When can a singular space become smooth, and how do its curve counts change?

A singular threefold can be smoothable at every singular point and still admit no smoothing at all. The papers determine when local smoothings coexist, how mirror symmetry transforms their relations, and how universal curve counts change through a transition. A compact counterexample identifies a limit to integer product expansions.

04

Geometry & moduli

How does geometry organize its possibilities?

As a bundle approaches a stability wall, its metric loses rigidity, and small eigenvalues record the scale of the loss. The papers connect stability chambers to spectra, metrics and geometric flows, and ask how wall crossing becomes geometry in open FJRW theory and which algebra a moduli space can realize.

05

Geometry, gravity & information

Which information does a physical description retain?

In a compact universe, singularities that could each change on their own may only change together; the papers work out which transitions the geometry allows. For black holes, they ask how much an entropy curve, even computed exactly, reveals about the quantum process behind it.

06

AI, institutions & power

What changes when intelligence becomes abundant?

Abundant analysis does not abolish the need for institutions, infrastructure or someone who answers for a decision; it moves the scarcity onto them. The papers follow that shift into markets between machines, the physical systems that sustain deployment, and the attribution of responsibility when decisions are delegated to algorithms.
