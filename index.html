Bernd Johannes Wuebben

Systematic Investing · Quantitative Research · Mathematics

I work on the cross-section of returns — how systematic signals are built, validated, and turned into portfolios — chiefly in corporate credit and fixed income, with a parallel interest in the spectral structure of factor models and in what statistical power implies about how much alpha can survive in equilibrium.

Separately, and for its own sake, I work in geometric topology and algebraic geometry: four-manifolds and instanton Floer theory, and the geometry of toric Fano varieties and Calabi–Yau threefolds.

Research

  • Systematic fixed income — credit factors, spread dynamics, duration-times-spread, portfolio construction under benchmark constraints.
  • Asset pricing — the spectral structure of the cross-section, identification of priced risk versus statistical alpha, the equilibrium level of factor compensation.
  • Portfolio methods — signal combination, shrinkage, and the continuum between diversification and mean–variance optimization.
  • Geometric topology — exotic smooth structures on four-manifolds, instanton knot homology, the Atiyah–Floer program.
  • Algebraic geometry — stability of tangent bundles on toric Fanos, deformations and smoothability of Calabi–Yau threefolds, open FJRW theory.

Papers

Mathematics

  • An exotic S²×S² and an exotic ℂP²#ℂ̄P² Reduces a homeomorphism question for Lidman–Piccirillo four-manifolds to the exotic S²×S² problem, with a certificate-complete computation of π₁ by coset enumeration and Knuth–Bendix completion.
  • The instanton homology of the (−2,3,q) pretzel knots and computed bounding cochains in the pillowcase The rank of instanton knot homology I♮ for the pretzel family, a computed deficiency law, and the first nonzero bounding cochains in pillowcase Floer theory. Companion paper: Traceless SU(2) characters and ℤ/4 instanton gradings for two-bridge and (3,n)-torus knots.
  • Slope stability of tangent bundles of smooth toric Fano varieties A reproducible Klyachko-criterion sweep with exact certificates over all 8,630 smooth toric Fano varieties in dimensions three through six.
  • Deformations of toric pairs and the smoothing of Batyrev Calabi–Yau threefolds With Doubly isolated Batyrev mirror pairs and non-smoothable Calabi–Yau threefolds: exact sweeps over the Kreuzer–Skarke reflexive-polytope classification, read through Batyrev mirror symmetry.
  • The central-charge threshold for wall-crossing in two-variable open FJRW theory A scattering-diagram structure theory for the Gross–Kelly–Tessler open enumerative invariants of xʳ + yˢ.

Quantitative finance

  • Where Alpha Resides: A Geometric View of Factor Models The triangular gauge of a factor model, the Sharpe ladder, and the factor zoo read as a spanning problem.
  • The Validation Bottleneck: Alpha Discovery When Hypotheses Are Free What happens to discovery when generating hypotheses costs nothing and only validation is scarce. Two pre-registered experiments on public data.
  • Beyond De Prado and Cotton: Hierarchical and Iterative Methods for General Mean-Variance Portfolios HRP-μ, HRP-Σμ, and the CRISP iterative shrinkage solver.
  • Spectral identification in the cross-section An ongoing program on spike-and-bulk structure, the alpha floor, and when a high-Sharpe model identifies priced risk rather than statistical alpha.
  • Duration times spread Empirical anatomy of spread-move proportionality in corporate credit and the elasticity correction it requires.
