Private design review · 25 October 2026

A complete proposal.
Every part is discussable.

This is a local working version. Nothing here has been pushed or published. Open the proposed site, compare it with the current version, and keep, revise, or leave out any of the six changes.

Explore the new homepage ↗Open the current site ↗

Review notes stay in this browser. They are not sent anywhere, and choosing “Keep” does not publish anything. Export notes if you want a separate copy.

Proposal 01

A homepage centered on the research ↗

An opening about the questions behind the work, direct and prominent access to the papers, then selected manuscripts and research programs. Essays and visual companions follow.

Does the opening have the right intellectual depth? Are the papers unmistakably central?

Proposal 02

A visual explorer gallery ↗

All four existing explorers, introduced through a question and a diagram. Their calculations and interactions are preserved.

Do the questions make you want to enter? Should the order or artwork change?

Proposal 03

Guides to the research ↗

Six program guides covering all 39 papers, with reading routes, scope notes, and expandable abstracts.

Are these useful groupings and entry points? We can keep individual guides independently.

Proposal 04

A paper catalogue organized by subject ↗

Subject navigation and larger descriptions, with direct access to every paper. Existing paper entries, PDF links, and archive links remain.

Does the catalogue remain comfortable to browse? Review the subject navigation and paper descriptions.

Proposal 05

A series of interactive essays ↗

Two complete new essay drafts alongside Not Enough and Gradient of Mind. Both new experiments use explicit illustrative assumptions.

Review the institutional-knowledge and physical-limits essays separately: argument, tone, calculation, and visual experience.

Proposal 06

About, updates, and RSS ↗

The fuller introduction has a home on About; contact uses your existing LinkedIn. Updates and RSS contain verified existing releases.

How much personal context belongs here? Is LinkedIn the right contact route? What deserves an update?

Notes save as you type.

The two new essays are drafts for discussion. New illustrations are schematic; their captions and the experiments state what is being represented. Manuscript PDFs and bibliographic records are unchanged. The shared navigation and stylesheet are common dependencies when adopting selected pages.
