The bundle, the masses, and the approximation

A K3 surface is a compact space of four real dimensions with special geometric structure. A bundle adds internal vector spaces to its points. Near two identical stable rank-three factors F ⊕ F, the multiplicity symmetry is SU(2). Two orthonormal harmonic deformation coordinates have amplitudes a and b, with 0 < b ≤ a. These are bundle deformations; no stability-wall parameter or singular K3 metric is being varied.

(m̂₁², m̂₂², m̂₃²) = [8 / (3V₀L²)] (b², a², a² + b²)

The actual leading Yang–Mills masses have relative error O(a² + b²), uniformly as b/a becomes small. V₀ is the reference K3 volume and L sets its physical size. The page draws the leading quantities m̂, suppressing the hats in the controls. Higher string-scale corrections are outside this model. The exact enhancement point a = b = 0 is approached but is not part of the positive-parameter controls.

The amplitude and its permitted domain

Here e² is the effective gauge-coupling prefactor. With x = E/M, μ = m₁/M = b/a, and c = cos θ, the dimensionless Mandelstam invariants are s = x², t = −s(1 − c)/2 and u = −s(1 + c)/2. They obey s + t + u = 0. The leading model evaluates

𝓜₀/e² = (t − u)/(μ² − s) + (t − s)/(μ² − u).

At low energy the denominators can be replaced by μ², giving (2t − u − s)/μ². The two curves compare these explicit formulas. They are not a measured cross-section or a numerical sum over the full internal spectrum.

The paper compares this expression with the complete leading Yang–Mills four-scalar amplitude, including the internal vector and scalar towers. Here the permitted comparison region uses b/a ≤ 0.4, E²/M² ≤ 0.5, and |μ² − q²| ≥ 0.15(μ² + x²) for q² = s,t,u. These are fixed illustrative choices within the theorem’s domain. The model suppresses the amplitude outside it and never connects a curve through a pole.

The theorem’s error is absolute and uniform in b/a in this domain, for sufficiently small a and b. No numerical constant is supplied, so no numerical uncertainty band is drawn. This does not establish a finite-string-scale error bound for the full heterotic amplitude.
